# DFW After Dark — Monday, November 9 through Sunday, November 15, 2026

All times Central Standard Time (America/Chicago, UTC−06:00). A seven-day guide emphasizing McKinney and North Dallas with selected Fort Worth and Dallas trips. Event listings, fees and availability can change: use the linked organizer before going.

## Three especially good plans

1. **Friday, Nov 13: Tony Stride jazz, 6–8 PM** — An intimate live-jazz concert in Heard-Craig's historic mansion ($15, reservations requested). [Official listing](https://www.visitmckinney.com/events/jazz-showcase-with-tony-stride-ut8libdo).

2. **Saturday, Nov 14: McKinney artists' day** — Browse Chestnut Square Farmers Market (8 AM–noon), MillHouse Makers Market (10 AM–4 PM, live music noon–3 PM), then the free McKinney Art Studio Tour (10 AM–6 PM). [MAST information](https://www.mastmckinney.org/), [MillHouse organizer](https://millhousefoundation.org/makers-market).

3. **Sunday, Nov 15: choose a market and music** — The Boardwalk Sunday Brunch Market in Plano (11 AM–3 PM), free community-band performance at 2:30 PM, or Fort Worth Greek Festival (11 AM–3 PM). [Boardwalk calendar](https://events.visitplano.com/event/sunday-brunch-market-2026), [community band](https://events.visitplano.com/event/breaking-the-sound-barrier), [Greek festival](https://www.fortworthgreekfestival.com/).
