## Publishing and data notes

- This edition starts **Wednesday, Nov 11** and ends **Tuesday, Nov 17**, inclusive. Events solely before Nov 11 are not in the active feed.
- Data feed: `data/events.json`. Each event includes its date, Central Time ISO offset, organizer link, venue and address, category, price and explicit unknown location coordinates.
- Some experiences (including holiday exhibits, markets and recurring studio events) have multiple date-specific records; they are not different attractions. A listed admission fee does not establish remaining ticket inventory.
- For events with missing session hours, the listing deliberately says **Hours: See organizer** rather than implying midnight operating hours.
- The site continues to display the separate `data/places.json` anytime destinations; no edits to those records were necessary.
