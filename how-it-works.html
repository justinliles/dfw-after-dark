GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED.

Is the website free to use?

Yes. Browsing, searching, saving locally and using the map are free. Some individual events charge admission or require tickets, which are purchased through the organizer.

How often do events change?

The guide is curated through a daily ChatGPT research workflow and published through GitHub and Netlify. Event details can change after publication, so always check the organizer's page before you go. If an update fails, the site may temporarily show older listings.

Does “removed” mean cancelled?

No. Removed means the listing left the rolling event feed. It might have expired, moved outside the seven-day window or been revised. It does not establish that the organizer cancelled it.

Does the website book tickets for me?

No. It sends you to the event organizer's page so you can check admission, availability and any age requirements before booking.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. Saved events are kept in your browser when storage is available, so they may not transfer to another device or persist in private browsing.

What if the event is not on the map?

Only listings with usable map coordinates appear as pins. You can still open the organizer's event link or directions when an address is provided.
